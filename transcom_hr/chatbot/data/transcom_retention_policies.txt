TRANSCOM ELECTRONICS LIMITED - HR RETENTION POLICIES & STRATEGIES

=========================================
SECTION 1: OVERTIME FATIGUE COUNTERMEASURES
=========================================
Overview:
Field operations at Transcom Electronics can involve intensive shift work. Overtime hours exceeding 40 hours per month are identified as critical flight risks.

Burnout Signs:
Managers should monitor field officers for early signs of burnout, including:
- Frequent late arrivals or unplanned single-day leaves.
- Drop in performance ratings (e.g., from 4/5 down to 1/5 or 2/5).
- Decreased participation in team communications and check-ins.

Max Recommended Shifts & Rest Periods:
- Weekly Limit: Employees must not exceed 48 total working hours per week.
- Continuous Shifts: No employee should work more than 6 consecutive days without a mandatory 24-hour rest period.
- Daily Rest: A minimum of 11 consecutive hours of rest must be guaranteed between the end of one shift and the beginning of the next.

Rota Planning & Comp-Time:
- Overtime cap: Under no circumstances should overtime exceed 50 hours per month.
- Compensatory Time Off (Comp-Time): For every 8 hours of overtime accumulated above the 40-hour threshold, employees are entitled to 1 paid day of compensatory leave to be taken within 30 days.

=========================================
SECTION 2: COMMUTE & DISTANCE MITIGATION
=========================================
Overview:
A commute distance exceeding 25 km from the workplace is a major driver of attrition due to physical exhaustion and travel costs.

Travel Stipends:
- Long Commute Allowance: Employees residing more than 20 km from their designated hub are eligible for a monthly travel stipend of 3,500 BDT.
- Shuttle Services: Shared microbus routing will be provided for high-density hubs in Dhaka and Chittagong.

Regional Territory Reassignments:
- Relocation Request: Field officers residing far from their current assignment can request lateral transfer to the nearest retail outlet or service hub.
- Processing timeline: Transfer requests must be reviewed and processed by regional HR within 14 business days.

Partial Remote Planning:
- Hybrid Roster: For non-outlet staff and support teams, a partial remote roster is allowed, permitting up to 2 days of work-from-home per week, subject to manager approval.

=========================================
SECTION 3: LOW ENGAGEMENT & MANAGER FRICTION
=========================================
Overview:
Manager effectiveness scores below 2.5/10 and employee engagement scores below 3/10 are major indicators of interpersonal friction and high flight risk.

Structured Feedback Intervals:
- Stay Interviews: Managers must conduct quarterly "Stay Interviews" with all medium and high-risk employees.
- Feedback Loops: One-on-one alignment sessions must occur bi-weekly for 30 minutes, focusing on career mapping rather than active task tracking.

Conflict Resolution Steps:
1. Informal Discussion: Address feedback directly between the manager and employee in a neutral setting.
2. Mediated Review: If friction persists, regional HR will step in to mediate and formulate a performance/communication agreement.
3. Outlet Reassignment: If resolution cannot be reached, the employee may be laterally reassigned to a different outlet/manager.

Leadership Coaching Template:
Managers with average team effectiveness scores under 5/10 must undergo the "Empathetic Leadership at Transcom" coaching program, which includes:
- Active listening training (3 modules).
- Constructive feedback framework (SBI: Situation, Behavior, Impact).
- Recognition practices (weekly shoutouts, performance rewards).
